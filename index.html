What if there was a place where you didn’t need expensive equipment, a private studio, or connections to start creating?

A place where you could learn from artists, meet people with similar interests, access creative tools, and develop your own projects.

CREATED TO CREATE is built around that idea.

This is not a traditional art school.

It is a combination of:

classroom
gallery
studio
workshop
stage
community

a space for CREATION

A place to come after school or university.

A place to draw, design, photograph, record, write, rehearse, experiment, or simply meet other creative people.

the center would also host:

screenings
performances
exhibitions
workshops
concerts
talks
festivals

The work created inside the center should not stay inside its walls.

It should be seen, heard, performed, exhibited, and shared

BEYOND THE WALLS

CREATED TO CREATE
is not limited to one building.

The goal is to eventually bring workshops, screenings, performances, and educational programs to communities outside Niš — especially to young people with limited access to creative education and resources.

CREATIVITY SHOULD TRAVEL.
